# Final Presentation – Remote Sensing AI Class  
### **LoRA Fine-Tuning Project – End of Semester Showcase**  
**Date:** 10 December  
**Format:** Team Presentation + Live Model Testing

---

## 1. Overview
Each team will finalize and present their own **LLM + LoRA adapter**, trained incrementally from Week 1 until **10 December**.  
During the final presentation, each team will:

- Showcase their custom AI assistant  
- Explain their LoRA training workflow  
- Demonstrate incremental training progress  
- Test their model live in front of the class  

---

## 2. Final Deliverables

### **Each team must prepare:**
- A complete **LoRA adapter** (final version)  
- A working **base LLM model**  
- A full **incremental training history** (Week 1 → Week 10)  
- A **15-minute presentation**  
- A laptop with **WebUI installed**  
- All models + LoRA adapters loaded in WebUI  
- Ability to test the model **offline** during the presentation  

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### **3.1 Continue Incremental Training Until 10 December**
- Continue adding new data each week  
- Maintain replay sets (10–30%)  
- Prevent drift using Week 8 techniques  
- Your final LoRA should be stable and domain-specialized  

### **3.2 Install WebUI + Models**
Each team must bring a laptop with:

- WebUI fully installed  
- Base LLM downloaded  
- Final LoRA adapter installed  
- Model successfully loaded and tested inside WebUI  
- No internet required for running the model  
- Fast and stable responses for LIVE testing  

---

## 4. Presentation Format (15 Minutes)

Each team receives **15 minutes** to present:

### **4.1 Introduction**
- Team name  
- Member roles  
- Model objective / target domain  

### **4.2 LoRA Training Process**
- How dataset was built  
- Replay strategy  
- Hyperparameter choices  
- Incremental training timeline  
- How drift was prevented  

### **4.3 Model Capabilities**
- What the model can do  
- Example prompts  
- Strengths and limitations  

### **4.4 Challenges & Solutions**
- Problems faced  
- How they were fixed  
- Improvements over time  

### **4.5 Demo Preparation**
- Show model ready inside WebUI  
- Confirm LoRA adapter loaded  

---

## 5. Live Testing (5 Questions)

After each presentation:

- Instructor will ask **5 questions** designed for the team’s model specialty  
- Team must answer using **their own WebUI + LoRA**  
- Answers must appear **LIVE** on screen  

### **Example question types:**
- Geology: mineral classification, lithology reasoning  
- Hyperspectral: band math, SWIR interpretation  
- Python: Random Forest GUI, error fixing  
- Coding: writing a function or class  
- General ML: explaining PCA, SVM, CNN steps  

---

## 6. Peer Grading

All other teams will grade the presenting team based on:

- **Accuracy** of responses  
- **Stability** of LoRA performance  
- **Specificity** to the chosen domain  
- **Speed** of response  
- **Quality** of presentation  
- **Depth** of incremental learning  

Scores will be collected immediately after each team finishes.

---

## 7. Prize for Best Team

The team with the **highest overall score** (presentation + live testing + peer evaluation) will receive:

- 🏆 **A special prize**  
- 🎉 Recognition as **Best AI Assistant Team of the Semester**
